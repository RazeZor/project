Sobre mi

Alejandro Fotografia

Fotografo

With over 10 years of experience capturing life's most precious moments, I bring a unique perspective and artistic vision to every shoot. My passion for photography began when I received my first camera at age 16, and has evolved into a lifelong pursuit of visual storytelling.

I specialize in wedding photography and special events, with a style that blends candid moments with artfully composed portraits. My approach is unobtrusive yet engaged, ensuring I capture authentic emotions while making everyone feel comfortable in front of the camera.

When I'm not behind the lens, you can find me exploring nature trails, experimenting with new cooking recipes, or spending time with my family.
